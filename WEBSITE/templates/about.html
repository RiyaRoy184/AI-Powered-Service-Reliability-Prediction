AI-Powered Service Reliability Prediction

A smart healthcare system designed to analyze medical equipment information and predict potential service reliability risks using machine learning. The system provides a risk classification, reliability score, and maintenance recommendation to support proactive maintenance of healthcare equipment.

Project Overview

Healthcare facilities depend on medical devices such as MRI scanners, CT scanners, X-ray machines, defibrillators, infusion pumps, and PET scanners. Equipment failure can interrupt healthcare services and require unexpected maintenance.

This project applies machine learning to historical equipment-related data to estimate the reliability risk of a device. The prediction is presented through a web-based interface so that users can easily enter equipment information and view the generated result.

Problem Statement

Traditional maintenance practices may depend heavily on fixed schedules or responding after equipment problems occur. Such approaches can make it difficult to identify devices that require attention earlier.

The proposed system uses equipment characteristics and maintenance-related parameters to estimate potential risk and provide a preventive maintenance recommendation.

Project Objectives

  • Predict the service reliability risk of healthcare equipment.
  • Classify equipment into Low, Medium, and High risk categories.
  • Generate an understandable reliability score for each prediction.
  • Provide a maintenance recommendation based on the predicted risk.
  • Provide a simple web interface for healthcare equipment assessment.

How the System Works

1
User Input

Equipment and maintenance information is entered through the prediction form.

2
Preprocessing

Input values are transformed using the preprocessing and encoding process used during training.

3
ML Prediction

The trained machine-learning model analyzes the equipment characteristics.

4
Risk Analysis

The device is classified into Low, Medium, or High Risk.

5
Recommendation

The system displays reliability information and a maintenance recommendation.

Risk Classification

Low Risk

The equipment is classified as having a lower predicted maintenance risk. Routine monitoring is recommended.

Medium Risk

The equipment shows a moderate predicted risk. Preventive maintenance should be scheduled soon.

High Risk

The equipment has a high predicted risk. Immediate inspection and maintenance are recommended.

Project Significance

The system demonstrates how machine learning can be integrated with a web-based healthcare application to support equipment reliability assessment. Instead of presenting only a machine learning prediction, the application converts the prediction into an understandable risk level, reliability score, and maintenance recommendation for easier interpretation.
